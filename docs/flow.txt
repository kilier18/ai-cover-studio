AI Cover Studio - pipeline
==========================

  song.mp3
     |
     v
 [1] separate.py        (python-audio-separator; BS-Roformer / Mel-Band RoFormer)
     |--> vocals.wav          lead vocal (optionally: strip backing vocals,
     |                        remove reverb, backfill ambience into the inst.)
     |--> instrumental.wav    accompaniment
     |
     v
 [2] rvc_convert.py     (isolated copy of the RVC kernel, its own python 3.9)
     |--> converted vocal (index_rate / protect / rms_mix / pre_gain / f0 ...)
     |
     v
 [3] mix.py             (numpy + librosa + ffmpeg)
     |--> final.mp3 / wav / flac / m4a   (gains, soft limiter, optional reverb)

work/<song>/ holds every intermediate file; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
